Project Purpose
=============================================================================

EmoCog is a web platform for cognitive research: capturing multimodal signals
in a browser (gaze, pulse, emotions, reaction time), server aggregation with
quality control, protocol constructor, and analytics for researchers.

Language Composition:
  - JavaScript: 49.8%
  - HTML: 43.9%
  - Python: 3.4%
  - TypeScript: 1.5%
  - CSS: 1.4%
